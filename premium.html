On supported calculators, Premium members can click "Explain my results" to get a plain-language summary of your numbers. You can also type follow-up questions in the same window—for example, "What if I claimed at 70 instead?" Explanations are educational only, not personalized financial advice.

Do the free calculators stay free?

Absolutely. All free calculators remain available. Premium adds extra features on supported tools.

Can I cancel anytime?

Manage or cancel your subscription through the account dashboard. Billing changes are handled securely through Stripe.

What if I don't want to subscribe?

No problem! The free versions are always available and will continue to receive updates. Premium is entirely optional.

Do you offer refunds?

If you are not satisfied within 30 days of your first payment, contact support to request a refund.

Is my data secure?

Your saved scenarios are stored in your account database. Payment details are handled by Stripe and are never stored on this site. You can delete saved scenarios, and you can contact support for account or data deletion requests.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through Stripe, our secure payment processor. Your card information never touches our servers.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Subscription changes are managed through Stripe from your account dashboard, where available.

How do I access premium features after subscribing?

After subscribing, simply log in to your account. Premium features will be unlocked on supported calculators and planning tools.
